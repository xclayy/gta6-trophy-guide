      Missable & timing

      Only one trophy is flagged as possibly missable by the guides that published the full list on 18 November 2026 (PowerPyx and GTABase, as reported by EGamers.io and KeenGamer). Everything else can be finished after the story, in free roam.

      Timing notes, not missables

      • Side-character chains (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone) — finish each strand as its missions appear. TechWiser, as reported by EGamers.io, notes the stranger chains behind five hidden trophies have windows tied to story progress, so do not leave a strand half-finished until after the credits.
      • Bank vaults — vaults open with an optional Chapter 4 mission. Take that mission when it appears; the vaults themselves stay open afterwards.
      • Snap Gun for Sneakthief — finish the Chapter 3 mission Yasmani's Jewelry Jobs to unlock the Snap Gun before attempting a silent jewelry robbery.
      • Cheats — using cheats disables trophies and saving for that session (GTABase and TechWiser, as reported by EGamers.io). Leave cheats off for the whole run.
